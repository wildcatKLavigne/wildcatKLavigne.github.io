Section 1  ·  Pre-Tournament Strategy

Plan Before You Play

Remember the scoring values: Single Delivery = 1 pt, Double Delivery = 3 pts, Continuous Control Bonus = 2 pts (stacks with delivery points). A clean double delivery with continuous control is worth 5 points in one action — nearly as much as five single deliveries without the bonus.

1. Given the scoring values above, what is your team's primary strategy — maximize the number of deliveries, or maximize double-deliveries with continuous control? Explain your reasoning.

2. Because the challenge winner is decided by total accumulated points (not total wins), how does this change how your team should play a match it is already winning by a wide margin in the final 15 seconds?

3. What is your team's plan for staying under the two-element control limit and avoiding an Excess Control Violation?
