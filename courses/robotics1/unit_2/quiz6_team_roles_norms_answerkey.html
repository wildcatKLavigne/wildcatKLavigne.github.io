Part A — Fill in the Blank Answers
Team Roles & Responsibilities
1 A role is an ongoing responsibility you own for the life of a project — not a one-time job — and creates accountability when something goes wrong.
Both terms needed for full credit (4 pts each). Accept "accountability" for the second blank only.
2 Technical and design decisions belong to the Lead Engineer, who has final say on anything involving the design.
3 The Project Manager owns the schedule and Gantt chart, runs team check-ins, and makes the call on non-technical decisions.
4 The Documenter / Analyst maintains the engineering notebook in real time, records test data and iteration decisions, and logs all trial times and scores.
Accept "Documenter" alone. Do not accept "Analyst" alone — this role title is always paired.
5 The Builder / Fabricator is responsible for build quality and fit, reports interface problems to the Lead Engineer immediately, and notifies the PM if the build is running behind.
Accept "Builder" alone.
Team Charter & Norms
6 A team charter is a written agreement about how a team operates — covering communication norms, decision-making, and accountability — drafted before any work begins.
7 The most critical section of a team charter is the decision-making protocol, which defines who makes which type of decision and what happens when team members disagree.
Must include both words for full credit. Do not accept "protocol" alone.
Mini Challenge & Concept Generation
8 Before selecting a design to build, each team generates three concept sketches showing different approaches.
Accept "concept sketches" or "concept sketch." Do not accept "sketch" alone — the compound term is the vocabulary introduced in the lesson.
Part B — Short Answer Rubrics
9 Bracket fit scenario — who owns the decision?
Scoring Rubric (6 pts)
3 ptsCorrectly names Lead Engineer as the role that owns this decision. (A bracket fit problem is a technical/design issue — not a schedule or documentation issue.)
3 ptsAction is specific and role-appropriate. Full credit examples: "Assess whether the gap affects function; decide to stop and add a shim or note it for iteration after the next trial." "Look at the interface, decide if it causes torque wobble, then tell the Builder what to fix or whether to proceed." Partial credit (1–2 pts): identifies the correct role but action is vague ("they should fix it") or incomplete.
Common wrong answers: "Project Manager" (scheduling, not design) · "Builder" (they report the issue; they don't decide the design response) · "The whole team" (no defined protocol for this — Lead Engineer owns technical calls).
10 6-inch extension limit — vocabulary and reasoning
Scoring Rubric (6 pts)
2 ptsNames the 6-inch limit as a constraint (Unit 1 vocabulary). Do not accept "rule" or "requirement" alone — the specific term from the course is "constraint."
4 ptsExplanation of why early checking saves time: must include the idea that discovering a constraint violation at the concept sketch stage requires only redrawing a sketch, while discovering it after building requires dismantling and rebuilding the module. Full credit requires both: (a) what happens if you check early, and (b) what happens if you don't. Partial credit (2 pts): only one of the two sides addressed.
Model answer: "The 6-inch limit is called a constraint. Checking for it during concept sketching saves time because if a concept violates it, you only need to draw a new sketch — no parts wasted. If you discover the violation after building, you have to dismantle the module and start the build over, which could cost a full class period."
Grading Notes Q1 has two blanks — award 4 pts per blank (8 pts total). If a student writes only one answer, score only the blank it fits.
Q2–Q8 are single-blank, 8 pts each — no partial credit on fill-in-the-blank unless noted above.
Q9–Q10 use the rubric above — partial credit is available. Encourage teachers to write brief feedback comments so students understand what the correct answer requires.
Suggested return policy: Return quizzes the following class period. Students who score below 60% should redo Q9 or Q10 with corrections before Class 7 to ensure they carry the role vocabulary forward into the Gantt/decision matrix lesson.
