WBS, Gantt & Critical Path
1 A breaks a project into phases, tasks, and subtasks until every piece of work is small enough that one person can own it, estimate it, and complete it.
2 A puts each task on a timeline so the whole team can see what is happening now, what is next, and which tasks are at risk of making the project late.
3 When Task B cannot start until Task A is finished, the relationship between them is called a . A chain of these relationships that determines the total project duration is called the .

Decision Matrix & Design Specification
6 In a weighted decision matrix, a is a number assigned to each criterion before scoring begins. It reflects how important that criterion is relative to the others. It must be set before any concept scores are entered.
