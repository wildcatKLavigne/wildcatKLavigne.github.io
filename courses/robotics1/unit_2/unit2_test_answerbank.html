Task 1. Risk Register & Partial Gantt Chart for a Novel Scenario (50 pts)

Scenario: A different robotics team is designing a brand-new attachment: a "flag deployer" module that must raise a small flag to a height of 12 inches within the last 10 seconds of a match. The module attaches to their robot using REV hardware, must not exceed a 4-inch footprint, and must be triggered by a single button press from the driver. The team has one 74-minute class period to design, build, and test this module before their first competition match.

Your task: Using everything you learned in Unit 2, build a risk register for this new project and create a partial Gantt chart showing how the team should plan their single class period.

Grading criteria — Task 1
  • Risk register: at least 6 distinct risks identified across at least 3 categories (design, parts, build, driver, or time) — 12 pts
  • Risk register: likelihood and impact correctly scored (1–3 each) and priority score correctly calculated (L × I) for every risk — 10 pts
  • Risk register: every risk with a priority score of 6 or higher has a specific, actionable mitigation strategy (not "hope it doesn't happen") — 10 pts
  • Gantt chart: at least 4 phases or tasks placed on a timeline within the single 74-minute period, with at least 1 milestone marked — 10 pts
  • Written justification: explains why one specific risk is the highest priority and how it shaped the Gantt chart's timing — 8 pts
